Houston Files: We Review Vehicle Records

When you ring, we ask about the vehicle, the purchase or lease, the repair visits, and the warranty. We can usually sort out the basic timeline and identify documents to gather during that conversation. We need repair orders, invoices, and correspondence in front of us to review specific entries.

We review files for new and used vehicles, whether bought or leased, with mileage from work routes or school runs. The purchase date, lease papers, odometer reading, warranty terms, and repair history can change the records placed in the file. We serve Houston, West University Place, Southside Place, and Bellaire.

At the end of a matter, we explain the status of the matter and the next available step using the repair record and correspondence. We hand back copies of the repair orders, correspondence, and notes kept with the file. The owner can then decide what to do with the records.
